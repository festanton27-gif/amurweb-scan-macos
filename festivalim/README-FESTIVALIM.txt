Festivalim DEV 0.2.0

New Match-3 foundation inspired by and adapted from Kenney Starter Kit Match-3 (MIT).
Festivalim branding, UI, level logic, objectives, hints, touch handling and visual assets are customized for this project.

Prototype level:
- 7x7 board
- 6 Festivalim tile types
- swipe or tap+tap control
- 12 hearts objective
- 18 moves
- automatic hint after inactivity
- win/lose flow
- Android portrait mode
